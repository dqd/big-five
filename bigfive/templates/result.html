{% blocktranslate with created=answer.created|date %}This report, generated on {{ created }}, estimates your level on each of the five broad personality domains of the Big Five model.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %}Personality traits describe how often or how intensely a person feels, thinks, or behaves in a certain way, relative to other people. Having a trait is therefore a matter of degree. We might call two people extraverts and still see one of them as more extraverted than the other. This report uses expressions such as “extravert” or “high in extraversion” for someone who is likely to be seen by others as relatively extraverted.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate with sex=answer.get_sex_display|lower age_group=answer.get_age_group_display %}The program that generates this report classifies you as low, average, or high in a trait according to whether your score falls roughly in the lowest 30%, the middle 40%, or the highest 30% of the scores of people of your sex and about your age ({{ sex }}, {{ age_group }}). Your scores are reported and charted as percentile estimates. For example, a score of 60 means that your level of that trait is estimated to be higher than that of 60% of people of your sex and age.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %}Please keep in mind that “low”, “average”, and “high” scores on a personality test are neither good nor bad in themselves. A particular level of any trait will probably be neutral or irrelevant for a great many activities, helpful for some, and a hindrance for others.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate %}As with any personality inventory, scores and descriptions can only approximate your actual personality. Descriptions of high and low scores are usually accurate, but an average score close to the low or high boundary might misclassify you as merely average. It is somewhat uncommon, but certainly possible, to score high in some of the six facets of a domain and low in others. In such cases the facet scores deserve more attention than the broad domain score. The best way to check the accuracy of your results is to show this report to people who know you well.{% endblocktranslate %}
